Rounds 7 and 8 shipped together and share a column. A `–` means the round did not score that view. Theme became a scored row in round 3 and its numbers come from the T column. Older docs named some views differently. Settings › Game was the Game link dialog, Backups was Recover, the start screen was the project chooser, and Keyboard shortcuts was the keybind cheat sheet.

## What each round changed

1. **Round 1, Overall 4.** The baseline on `feat/workspace` had no type scale, one grey for everything, four radii and developer vocabulary on every surface. Light scored 2 because half the chrome stayed dark.
2. **Round 2, Overall 6.** The type scale, greys and radii landed. Friendly names replaced class names, Save, Rename and Duplicate became one-field dialogs, a start screen replaced the chooser modal, and Settings › Game replaced the Game link wall.
3. **Round 3, Overall 5.** The round 2 input bugs went, Display became the one home for its toggles, and Workshop moved under its map. The new palette failed. The owner rejected the purple-navy and yellow, Theme scored 2, and Overall fell a point.
4. **Round 4, Overall 7.** The theme reset brought neutral charcoal surfaces, one teal accent and alpha hairlines. Sky off stopped drawing a dome, and Outline rows got thumbnails.
5. **Round 5, Overall 8.** Light became a real theme with transparent thumbnails, a lighter grid and water that fades into the backdrop. Untouched maps stayed clean and a green Saved chip arrived. The owner then raised the bar for dark, which scored 6 against it.
6. **Round 6, Overall 8.** Dark moved to a near-black base with three readable levels and saturated status pills, and Theme reached 8. Light ortho panes took a tint from the water. Top bar, Dock, Menus, Settings dialog and Start screen reached 9.
7. **Round 7, scored with round 8.** All six round 6 next steps landed, among them dark water that runs into the haze with no band, opaque popovers, a first placed piece that lands in view, and a chip that follows the dirty flag at once.
8. **Round 8, Overall 9.** 46 of the 48 "8 to 9" items checked out, including scrub and relative entry in number fields, drag-to-place, a view cube, backup diffs and buffered project settings. Theme reached 9. Split panes and Light theme stayed at 8.
9. **Round 9, Overall 9.** Split panes and Light theme reached 9, so no view sits below 9. A maximised pane now says so and offers Esc to restore, and light has its three surface levels with disabled text at 3.11:1 on cards.
10. **Open after round 9.** The Save, Rename, Duplicate and New project dialogs still spellcheck the name, and in light the Not exported badge disappears on the active project row. [ui-critique-round-9.md](ui-critique-round-9.md) lists the rest.
